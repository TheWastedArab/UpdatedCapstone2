## What the study is about

This study checks whether a new user can learn to use a bug tracker inside VS Code quickly.
We are testing the software, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

## What you will do

You will do five short tasks in VS Code, such as creating an account and reporting a
made-up bug, while saying aloud what you are thinking. Afterwards you will fill in a
ten-question form about how easy the software was to use, and answer a few questions.
The whole session takes about 25 minutes.

## Your data

- We record how long each task takes, whether it was completed, and your comments.
- You are identified only by a code (for example P3). Your name is on this form only, and
  this form is stored separately from the results.
- The email address you use in the tasks is a made-up study address, not your own.
- The screen is recorded only if you tick the box below. Recordings are deleted once the
  results have been written down, and no later than the end of the project.
- Anonymous results may appear in the project report.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time, without giving a reason, and ask for
your results to be removed up to the date the report is submitted.
